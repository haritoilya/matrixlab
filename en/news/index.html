    11 July 2023
    Use of fish ELASTIN hydrolysate to enhance fibroblast proliferation, elastin synthesis and improve human skin condition
    According to recent studies, certain peptides significantly improve the skin condition of healthy women, increasing its elasticity and moisture content.

    The aim of this study was to examine the effect of elastin hydrolysate on human skin. Proliferation and elastin synthesis were assessed in human skin fibroblasts in the laboratory after exposure to elastin hydrolysate and to prolyl-glycine, which is found in human blood after oral intake of elastin hydrolysate.

    Oral intake of elastin hydrolysate improved skin condition, increased its elasticity, reduced wrinkles and enhanced blood circulation.

    (Published in Universum, an international scientific journal, in the issue on Medicine and Pharmacology)

    Elastin is a resource worth protecting
    Elastin deficiency leads to wrinkles and ptosis and makes scars visible. It is the protein that drives skin regeneration.

The authors hope that in the near future fish elastin hydrolysate will benefit people as a cosmetic and dietary supplement.
